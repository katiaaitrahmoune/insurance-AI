### COV-001: Accidental Collision Damage
The Insurer will pay for sudden, direct, and accidental physical damage to the Covered Vehicle caused by a Collision during the Policy Period.

### COV-002: Damage Caused by Another Vehicle
Coverage applies when the Covered Vehicle is struck by another vehicle, provided the impact constitutes a Collision and the Covered Vehicle was being used in accordance with policy terms.

### COV-003: Coverage Conditions

## Section 4 — Exclusions and Limitations

### EXC-001: Intentional Damage
No coverage is provided for any damage caused intentionally by the Insured or at the direction of the Insured. Evidence suggesting deliberate acts must be flagged for mandatory human review to determine if the intentional damage exclusion applies; the AI system shall not automatically deny the claim

### EXC-002: Fraudulent Information
Claims will be denied entirely if the Claimant provides materially misleading, fabricated, or altered evidence, including manipulated photographs or false statements regarding the circumstances of the Accident.

### EXC-003: Prior Damage Exclusion
No coverage is provided for Pre-existing Damage. The Insurer will only pay to repair damage newly created by the claimed Accident.

### EXC-004: Unapproved Commercial Use
Damage is excluded if the Covered Vehicle was being used for commercial ride-sharing, delivery services, or as a taxi at the time of the Accident.

### EXC-005: Consequential Loss
The Insurer will not pay for loss of use, depreciation in value after repairs, or alternative transportation costs.

## Section 5 — Coverage Limits and Deductibles

### LIM-001: Maximum Coverage Limit
The Maximum Coverage Limit per incident is $50,000. 

### LIM-002: Applicable Deductible
A fixed Deductible of $500 applies to every Covered Loss. This amount is subtracted from the final approved repair estimate.

### LIM-003: Calculation Example
The policy limit of $50,000 applies after the deductible is subtracted. For example, on a $55,000 repair estimate with a $500 deductible: The $500 deductible is applied first, leaving a $54,500 claim. The insurer pays the maximum limit of $50,000. The insured is responsible for the $500 deductible plus the remaining $4,500 (totaling $5,000 in out-of-pocket costs).

## Section 7 — Evidence Requirements

### EVD-001: Photographic Evidence
Photographs must clearly show the damage context. While photographs prove damage exists, they do not inherently prove when the damage occurred or that it resulted from the described Accident.

### EVD-002: Police and Third-Party Reports
Police reports or accident reports are required if another vehicle is involved. These documents are used to corroborate the date, time, and general circumstances of the Collision.

### EVD-003: Repair Estimates
All repair estimates must be itemized. The Insurer relies on these to calculate the total potential settlement but reserves the right to adjust estimates that include Pre-existing Damage.

### EVD-004: Evidentiary Limitations
Observed facts in evidence (e.g., "the bumper is dented") are distinct from coverage conclusions. The presence of damage does not automatically equate to a Covered Loss.

## Section 8 — Pre-Existing Damage

### PRE-001: Identification of Prior Damage
Rust, weathering, and paint-transfer differences are considered potential indicators of pre-existing damage, not definitive proof. The AI system cannot definitively establish when damage occurred based solely on photographic evidence. Claims flagged with these indicators require human review only when they materially affect the assessment of the claim's eligibility or settlement calculation.
### PRE-002: Overlapping Damage
If a new Covered Loss affects an area of the Covered Vehicle that already contained Pre-existing Damage, the Insurer will deduct the estimated cost to repair the old damage from the total settlement.

### PRE-003: Insufficient Evidence of Timing
If it is impossible to determine whether damage is new or pre-existing based on the submitted evidence, the claim requires mandatory human review by an authorized adjuster.
